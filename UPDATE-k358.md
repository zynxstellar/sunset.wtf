# Rift k358

Car Noclip now keeps car collisions disabled even during ram/contact attempts. Also disables collisions on the local seated driver and accessories, catches newly added parts, and restores original collision values on disable, seat loss, death, game change and unload. Other passengers are excluded.

Physical ramming cannot be relied on while passing through players. Local collision tests and full regression suite passed; server-side collision correction remains unverified.
